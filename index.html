About this data & methodology

This dashboard combines several public satellite and open-data sources to estimate urban heat risk for each ward in Mumbai:

  • MODIS Land Surface Temperature (LST) — satellite-derived surface temperature, the basis for the heat overlay and lst values.
  • Sentinel-2 NDVI — vegetation index used to estimate green cover per ward (ndvi).
  • WorldPop — gridded population density estimates (population_density_worldpop).
  • NASA POWER — near-surface air temperature reanalysis data (power_temp).
  • Census 2011 — official ward-level population figures (population_census).
  • ESA WorldCover — land cover classification used to estimate impervious surface share (impervious_pct).

Honest note: this shows the most recent available satellite pass and modeled estimates for each ward — it is not a literal real-time reading. Satellite revisit times mean the underlying imagery can be anywhere from hours to a couple of weeks old depending on cloud cover and source. Some fields may show "N/A" where a given data source did not have coverage for a ward.
